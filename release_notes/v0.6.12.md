# Kintsugi v0.6.12

## Terminal pane resizing

- Let interactive Work-view terminals fit their renderer grid after the pane changes size. The renderer's resize observer previously restored explicitly pinned startup rows and columns, undoing later fits.
- Keep the measured initial grid for early transport attachment. Read-only terminal-output blocks retain their explicit fixed grid.

Managed Chromium review reproduced the stale canvas and confirmed that dragging the Work pane divider now resizes it correctly. The eight-scenario partial, no-inference composer/layout acceptance passed. Desktop Safari and live PTY resizing have not been confirmed for this fix.

## Compatibility and channels

No protocol or saved-settings changes. System compatibility remains 7, the app minimum remains 0, and network/resource policy readers remain 2 and 3. The MCP settings-v2 downgrade restriction from v0.6.9 is unchanged.

This release advances `latest` and, after verification, `stable`. Publication does not deploy or restart an installation.
