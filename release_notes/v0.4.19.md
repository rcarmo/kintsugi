# Kintsugi v0.4.19

Adds two upstream skills and aligns `/review` with upstream's review-and-fix workflow.

- **Upstream PR:** prepare a minimal reproduction, useful before/after evidence and a maintainer-friendly fix. Personal introductions are optional and left to the human author; Lucas's mandatory first-person introduction is not copied. Opening a PR against upstream still requires an explicit request.
- **Omakase:** opt-in numbered proposals, rationale and alternatives, with a fallback HTML template. Adopted unchanged from upstream.
- **Review:** invoking `/review` now commits the scoped work before reviewing, fixes or pushes back on findings, creates one severity-labelled commit per fix and continues useful review rounds. This invocation authorises local commits and in-scope fixes, not pushes or deployments. Unrelated edits, test restrictions, stored-data/upgrade reporting and user-delivered Review boundaries remain protected.

The one-shot reviewer remains review-only; orchestration performs approved fixes and commits. Merely opening or saving a skill or prompt starts no work. No extra prompt-suggestion inference is introduced.

Adapted from Lucas Meijer's AgentsInTheCloud through `b244e676ac3b5fc620371b3da84adfc2988b1acb`. Attribution and MIT notices remain intact.

No dependency, runtime-image pin, compatibility-counter or persisted-state format changes. Verification uses full local checks and the tagged CI/native packaged System/app boot gates; no live provider inference or installation update is needed for this workflow-only patch.

Publication advances `latest` only; `stable` remains v0.4.10. No installation or workspace is updated or restarted by publication.
