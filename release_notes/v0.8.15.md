# Kintsugi v0.8.15

- **Work → Subagents** now lists every Agent in the workspace, regardless of which tab is selected, and nests delegated subagents under their parents. Each row shows its configured model and thinking level when available.
- `spawn_agent` accepts an optional `thinkingLevel`. Omit it to inherit the parent’s level. Unsupported explicit levels are rejected before a subagent is created, and the chosen level persists across follow-ups.
