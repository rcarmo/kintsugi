# Kintsugi v0.8.9

The Plan pane opens as a checklist with a progress bar and status icons that follow the active theme. Choose **Edit** to change the Markdown. Saving a plan does not send it to the Agent; **Submit to model** still does.

- Expanded tool output stays visible while the completed output loads. If loading fails, close and reopen the detail to retry.
- API-key validation consistently chooses a suitable chat model instead of picking one at random. Gemini Deep Research models are excluded from chat validation.
- Interrupted Tailscale Serve responses now fail promptly instead of leaving operations pending.
- The egress proxy can issue certificates for DNS names longer than 64 characters without changing how hostnames are verified.
