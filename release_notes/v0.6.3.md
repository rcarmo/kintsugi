- Summarise each optimizer operation in plain English, such as “Learned from new work · 1 new fact” or “Asked small-observer which tools this task needs · could hide 2 tools”.
- Keep optimizer rows collapsed by default, including while they run.
- When opened, show a readable table: what was checked, what was learned or decided, model usage and timing, and when changes take effect. Prompts and long evidence lists stay folded.
