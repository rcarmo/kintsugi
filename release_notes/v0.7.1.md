# Kintsugi v0.7.1

## Scrollback and live connections

- Fix automatic and button-driven loading of earlier messages, including pages containing only bookkeeping entries. Preserve the passage being read as history arrives.
- Give background/subagent transcripts their own scrolling and turn scope. Keep message-reveal targets visible while disclosures open and reflow.
- Apply the history viewer’s 200-entry/2 MiB page budget to live scrollback. An individually oversized entry remains readable as the minimum page unit.
- Prevent rapid WebSocket reconnect loops after short-lived handshakes. Use jittered backoff and reset it only after 10 seconds of settled connectivity.
- Pause background retries while offline and probe on online/wake events. Keep reachable local/tailnet apps usable despite unreliable offline hints; preserve the browser’s explicit SSE selection.

## Facts and timeline

- When the token optimizer is enabled, Basic now learns repository facts automatically from tool-backed evidence without extra model calls. **On** applies eligible facts on the next request, including during an ongoing session; **Watch only** records observations without changing context. Model-assisted writing stays within configured model and call limits. The optimizer remains experimental and off by default.
- Tighten collapsed tool rows and give saved-output reads/searches human-readable summaries, including line ranges, character offsets and search terms.

## Compatibility and channels

System compatibility remains 7, the app minimum remains 0, and network/resource policy readers remain 2 and 3. Custom release sources and image pins remain authoritative.

Publication advances `latest` only and leaves `stable` at v0.6.12. It does not deploy or restart an installation. Real-device Safari sleep/keyboard and real-model quality/cost acceptance remain separate checks.
